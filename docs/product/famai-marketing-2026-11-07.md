# FamAI and Mia standalone page

Public route: /fam-ai. Persuade surface for parents coordinating their household. It introduces Mia Voice as the personal AI home assistant and AI Chat as a separate text experience.

Visual world (revised): near-black #050506, graphite #111117, silver #f5f5f7, lilac #bab4f4 and soft blue #a2c7ff. Self-hosted General Sans. Oversized type, dimensional phone mockups, luminous animated voice sculpture, and a pinned scroll sequence that transforms from spoken request to event review. Voice/chat previews remain illustrative and never request microphone access or write family data. Pause controls and reduced-motion mode provide a complete static experience. The page is isolated from the generic landing-page class; a page-scoped document overflow rule defeats the app scroll-unfreeze guard only on this route, preserving drawer scroll locking.
