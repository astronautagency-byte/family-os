# Privacy operations — draft for adoption

Status: proposed workflow, not evidence of operation. No privacy lead, legal reviewer, provider contracts or retention schedules are deemed appointed/approved by this document.

## Rights requests

1. A designated lead monitors support@fam-os.app, records receipt, jurisdiction, request type, identity-check needs and due date in an access-restricted request log.
2. Verify identity proportionately through the signed-in account or another secure agreed channel. Do not request passwords or routine government-ID uploads by email/chat.
3. Map the person's records across household/shared data, AI history, object storage, integrations, notifications, delivery logs, billing, providers and backups. Separate another family member's data before export.
4. Assemble/review the response or document an applicable limitation with the reviewer. Where GDPR applies, ordinarily respond within one month and explain a permitted extension within that month. Confirm other jurisdiction-specific timelines separately.
5. Record correction/erasure/restriction actions and actual outcomes, exceptions, recipients notified and remaining backup/provider handling. A successful auth-user deletion alone is insufficient proof of full erasure.
6. Close with an understandable response, escalation/complaint options, and retention of only necessary request evidence. Test with synthetic data before treating the workflow as established.

## Incident handling

1. Assign an incident lead and backup contacts; maintain secure out-of-band access to the contact list.
2. Contain the issue, preserve restricted forensic evidence and record when the organization became aware. Avoid copying customer content into general AI tools or public trackers.
3. Assess affected systems, people, countries, data sensitivity, likely impacts and mitigations with the privacy lead and qualified reviewer.
4. Assess authority, individual and contractual notifications promptly. Under GDPR, a reportable breach generally has a 72-hour authority-notification window where feasible; not every incident is automatically reportable. Document the assessment and applicable exceptions/deadlines.
5. Track remediation, recovery, affected-party communications and a post-incident review. Run a tabletop exercise; store evidence securely. No messages are sent or notifications certified by this draft.

## Before signing the fundraising readiness gate

Assign owners and collect real evidence: accepted provider terms/DPAs and data settings, processing/retention register, children/sensitive-data assessment, a complete rights-request exercise, backup/file/provider erasure results, incident exercise, security triage/retest and legal review. Record explicit reviewer decisions and scope. Never pre-fill approval or dates for work not performed.
