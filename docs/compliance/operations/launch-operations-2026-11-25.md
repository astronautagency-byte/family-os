Alex is the incident lead. Preserve minimal evidence with timestamps, contain affected credentials/services, identify affected accounts and processors, assess harm with qualified advice, record notification decisions and deadlines, notify affected parties/regulators where required, and retain the incident record. - Weekly: review admin access, failed auth, usage/cost anomalies, pending erasure/rights requests and deployment failures. Retain actual evidence, not pre-completed checklists.
- Monthly: dependency advisories, processor/subprocessor changes, feature/data-flow review and entitlement reconciliation. Rotate credentials on exposure or authorized policy; never store server keys in browser bundles.
- Before restoring helpers: review all old invitations/grants, explicitly decide which may resume, retest access/expiry/tenant isolation, then restore grants and delivery. Never re-enable by merely revealing the UI.
- Before paid AI: verify displayed currency/limits, sandbox payment, signed/replayed/out-of-order webhooks, entitlement/cancellation/expiry and hard usage caps; then require an operator-performed live purchase check. Store purchases need their own implementation and verification.
