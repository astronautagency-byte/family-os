## Short description

Share your family calendar, grocery lists, tasks, and meal plans.

## Full description

Keep your household’s plans together in FamOS.

• Share a family calendar for appointments, practices and school activities.
• Organize chores and errands with shared task lists.
• Add groceries to a shared shopping list and check items off as you shop.
• Plan meals for the week and keep track of what you already have in the kitchen.
• Share updates with your household in family chat.

Sign in with your FamOS account to see the plans shared with your household.

## Release notes

The first FamOS release for Android brings shared calendars, task lists, grocery lists, meal planning and household chat together.
