## Description (4000 characters max)

**The family organizer that actually works.**

FamOS brings calendars, meals, groceries, tasks, chat, and AI assistance into one private space your whole household can share. No more juggling five different apps or losing track of who's doing what.

**Built for real family life.**

Whether you're coordinating school pickups, planning meals around dietary needs, or making sure everyone knows who's driving tonight — FamOS keeps your household running smoothly without the chaos.

**What makes FamOS different:**

• **Shared Calendar** — Bring in Google, Apple, or Outlook calendars. See everyone's schedule in one beautiful view. Know who's where, when.

• **Meal Planning** — Plan the week's dinners in minutes. Use the Roulette feature for meal ideas based on what's in your kitchen. Follow recipes hands-free with Cook Mode.

• **Smart Grocery Lists** — Quick-add items on the go. Focus mode for in-store shopping. Items auto-categorize by aisle.

• **Task Management** — Assign clear owners to every task. Custom lists for different areas of life. Routines for the stuff that repeats.

• **Family Chat** — Keep conversations and plans together. Share updates with the whole household or message specific people.

• **Kitchen Watch** — Track what's in your fridge, freezer, and pantry. Get reminders before things expire. Plan meals around what you already have.

• **Fam AI** — Ask for meal ideas, grocery lists, or help planning the week. Understands your household context and never changes anything without your approval.

**Privacy-first design.**

Your household has its own protected space. FamOS asks before AI actions are applied and keeps family coordination visible only to the people you invite.

**Works everywhere you do.**

FamOS runs on your Mac, iPhone, iPad, or any wall-mounted screen. Same household view on every device — no dedicated hardware required.

**Start free, grow when ready.**

Calendar, tasks, shopping, chat, and kitchen watch are free forever. Upgrade to Plus or Pro for sync, recipes, meal planning, and more.

Download FamOS and give your family the clarity it deserves.
