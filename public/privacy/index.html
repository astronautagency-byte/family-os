Information we collect and where it comes from

You provide account and profile details, household content, messages, lists, calendars, meals, preferences, invitations, support messages and files. Other household members may add information about you or invite you. Connected calendars and services supply information you authorize. We also process service and security metadata such as access times, device/browser details, delivery status and usage counts. Payment processors handle payment credentials; FamOS receives subscription, invoice and payment-status information rather than full card details.

Purposes and lawful bases

Where GDPR applies, we use information necessary to provide the service under our contract with an adult account holder; meet legal obligations such as required billing records; and pursue legitimate interests such as service security, fraud prevention and necessary household coordination, subject to your rights and applicable safeguards. Optional AI processing and location sharing are choices you can decline or withdraw. Consent is used where required; accepting the terms does not provide blanket consent for every use of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Children and household accounts

An adult must create and manage a household. A child or teen may use age-appropriate household features only when authorized by their parent or legal guardian and permitted by applicable law. Where consent is the legal basis for an online service offered directly to a child, the applicable digital-consent age varies by country, generally from 13 to 16 in the EEA. Parental authorization must meet local requirements; an invitation alone is not proof of legally valid parental consent. Children retain their own privacy rights. AI features are currently restricted to users aged 18 or older, with an adult declaration and checks against available household role and age information. This is not government-ID age verification.

AI Chat, Mia and optional AI processing

AI processing is off until an eligible adult chooses to enable it for their own account. That choice and its policy version are recorded. You can withdraw in Settings → Account → AI privacy or in the assistant’s AI privacy choices. Withdrawal stops future AI requests; it does not erase existing conversations, saved items or data already processed. Use the deletion-request process for those records.

The prompts, recent conversation turns, AI replies and images or other inputs you explicitly submit for AI processing may be sent to Groq or xAI. AI Chat does not automatically send a household snapshot, family-member list, location history, calendar contents or household notes. Information you include in your own messages or images can still identify people. Do not submit children’s personal information, medical details, precise locations, identity documents or other sensitive information. Review AI results before saving; an AI suggestion is not a completed action.

FamOS does not train its own general-purpose AI models on your household content. Providers’ retention, safety monitoring and contractual data controls are separate; we do not promise universal zero-data retention or that no provider can retain content. Contact us for information about the controls applying to a particular feature.

Microphone, speech and voice notes

Mia starts microphone access only when you choose a voice control and permit microphone access. Speech recognition is supplied by your browser or device and may send audio to its speech-service provider. Recognized text is used to prepare your request; reply text is sent for AI speech generation. Mia’s conversation audio is not saved as a voice-note recording by FamOS. End the conversation, close the panel or background the app to stop listening; inactivity can also pause it. Mia’s voice is AI-generated.

Family voice notes are separate: recordings you intentionally send are stored and shared with their intended household recipients. Automatic voice-note transcription and automatic inbound-email AI extraction are paused pending privacy review. Playing or deleting a voice note is separate from Mia’s speech recognition.

Helpers, invitations and shared content

Household members can see content according to their roles. Nannies, babysitters and housekeeping helpers use separate, limited access to selected lists, calendars or event resources; an invitation is not full family membership. New helper invitations can use a family-assigned passcode and a private link without creating a helper account. When requested with recipient permission, we process the mobile number to send the invitation by SMS through our delivery providers. We store a salted passcode hash, limited session credentials and access/security records; passcodes and private links must be kept confidential. Earlier email-bound invitations remain tied to the invited account. Invitations and access may expire. Authorized family members can revoke access or remove helpers. Revocation stops future access but cannot erase information someone already viewed, downloaded or copied. Event help planning does not itself confirm a service booking or authorize payment.

Location and check-ins

Location sharing requires a deliberate choice and device permission. In the current web app it updates while the location page is open, and stops updating when you leave that page or background the app. The latest location can remain visible for up to 30 minutes after its last update and is removed shortly after expiry or when sharing is stopped. A voluntary check-in is visible for 24 hours and is removed shortly after. Opening coordinates in Google Maps sends them to Google. FamOS is not an emergency or safety-monitoring service.

Calendars and other connected services

Connecting Google Calendar may authorize calendar-list access and event read/write access across calendars accessible to your account. FamOS displays and synchronizes the calendars you select and sends approved changes to writable connected calendars. Disconnect in Settings and revoke authorization with the provider when appropriate. Removing a connection does not necessarily delete imported copies or records you saved in either service. Other integrations process the information needed for the feature you choose and are subject to their own terms.

Current service categories include Supabase for the backend, Vercel for web hosting, Groq and xAI for AI requests, and calendar, email, payment and device/push providers where used. Google, Microsoft and other connected services receive data when you authorize the relevant connection. Browser speech providers may receive microphone audio independently of FamOS. We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to address abuse, security incidents or legal claims. Contact us for the current processor and integration inventory.

Storage on your device

FamOS uses browser or app storage for sign-in sessions, preferences, cached feature data and operation of the installed app. Clearing device storage or signing out can remove local copies but does not delete your cloud account. We do not treat agreement to these terms as consent to optional advertising or analytics tracking; any such tracking requiring consent must remain off until an appropriate choice is offered.

Retention and deletion

Household content is generally retained while needed for the account and the features you choose, unless you delete it or request erasure. AI Chat conversations saved to your history remain until deleted or removed with the account. Optional AI-processing choices are retained as evidence of your preferences while needed; voice-session audio and saved family voice notes are different categories. Limited billing, security or dispute records may need to remain longer to meet legal obligations or resolve claims.

You can delete your account in Settings. The effect on shared household records depends on your role and whether other members still need those records. Deleted information may remain temporarily in protected backups or provider systems under their retention rules, and information already shared with another person may remain with that person. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or third-party system. Contact us for a category-specific retention or erasure explanation.

AI proposals are subject to your review; FamOS is not intended to make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.
